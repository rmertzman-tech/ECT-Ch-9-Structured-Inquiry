What Price Is Missing From the Map?

Protect the conditions. What condition is being supported, damaged, hidden, or repaired?

How to use this

Live class: complete ENTER individually, compare in pairs/groups, and open each UNLOCK only when the instructor reaches it.

Online/self-paced: work individually and open the three UNLOCKS in order. At COMPARE, construct a strong alternative map rather than waiting for a group.

Target: about 35–45 minutes of active inquiry distributed across a 60–75 minute class, or about 30–40 minutes self-paced.

Cheap Housing, Dirty Air

A city approves a new affordable-housing development near an industrial area. Housing costs are rising, many families need places they can afford, and city leaders describe the project as an expansion of opportunity. For now, focus on that visible benefit.

Before discussion or chapter explanation, make your own first map.

Claim ceiling: your first reaction is data for inquiry, not a final verdict. Preserve it before later information changes the map.

Instructor + chapter
3 · UNLOCK — Teach Into the Case

Open each instrument when the chapter reaches it

These are not a mini-lecture replacing the chapter. They are short instruments for testing the map you already made.

UNLOCK 1 — Agency needs conditions
Instructor pause 1 · self-paced students open in order

The area already has poor air quality, heavy truck traffic, noise, and pollution. Some families report asthma and breathing problems.

Instrument: agency does not happen in empty space. Breathable air, clean water, stable shelter, health, land, climate, and functioning ecosystems are life-support conditions. Housing and breathable air are both conditions of agency.

UNLOCK 2 — Visible price vs hidden cost
Instructor pause 2 · self-paced students open in order

The rent may be lower, while health costs appear elsewhere: asthma, medical bills, missed school or work, stress, and long-term exposure. Some residents have few realistic housing alternatives and limited influence over siting decisions.

Instrument: a visible price can be low because part of the real cost is shifted onto bodies, communities, ecosystems, or futures. Environmental justice asks who benefits, who is burdened, who has voice, and who can refuse.

UNLOCK 3 — Future agency and protection
Instructor pause 3 · self-paced students open in order

Children and future residents may inherit health burdens and environmental conditions they did not choose. A responsible map therefore asks what protection, monitoring, redesign, participation, or restoration could keep both housing and health in view.

Instrument: future agency asks whether later people will retain conditions for living, choosing, participating, and repairing.
